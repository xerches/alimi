# Referral agreement: outline

*Structure and plain-language intent for the attorney who drafts the agreement. The fee and its basis are read from `config/pricing.yaml`. Clauses marked [Attorney review] need legal text.*

**Parties:** {{Alimi Entity}} ("Alimi") and {{Partner}} of {{Partner Firm}} ("Referrer"). **Effective date:** {{Effective Date}}.

## 1. Who this is for

{{pricing.partners.referral.who}}. The Referrer introduces clients who could use Alimi's products or services and does not resell or deliver them. A firm that wants to resell uses the reseller agreement instead.

## 2. What counts as a referral

A referral is a written introduction (an email naming the prospect, with the prospect copied or with their prior consent) of a company that is not already in Alimi's pipeline, which Alimi confirms within five business days. A referral stays credited to the Referrer for twelve months from the introduction. Prospects Alimi was already talking to are listed back to the Referrer at confirmation so there are no surprises.

## 3. The fee

Alimi pays the Referrer **{{pricing.partners.referral.fee}}** of {{pricing.partners.referral.of}}, paid {{pricing.partners.referral.paid}}. No fee is due on renewals, on later products the client buys after the first year, or on amounts refunded or unpaid. Alimi sends a statement with each payment showing the invoice it relates to.

## 4. Professional rules and disclosure

**[Attorney review]** Some referrers cannot accept a referral fee, or can only accept it with disclosure and consent, under their professional rules: attorneys under the Massachusetts Rules of Professional Conduct, insurance producers under anti-rebating and inducement rules, and CPAs under the AICPA code for certain clients. The agreement therefore offers each Referrer a choice at signing, recorded in Schedule A:

- **[ ]** Accept the fee as above, and disclose the arrangement to the referred client in writing before the introduction, in the form in Schedule B.
- **[ ]** Decline the fee. Alimi instead gives the referred client the same amount as a discount on its first invoice, and tells the client who referred them.
- **[ ]** Direct the fee to a charity named by the Referrer.

The Referrer confirms that its chosen option complies with the rules that apply to it, and may change the option for future referrals in writing.

## 5. What the Referrer does and does not do

The Referrer may describe Alimi's services using Alimi's current one-pager and may say that it refers clients to Alimi. The Referrer does not quote prices beyond the published list, make promises about insurance outcomes, audit results or breach prevention, negotiate on Alimi's behalf, or sign anything for Alimi. The Referrer is not Alimi's agent.

## 6. What Alimi does

Alimi responds to every referral within two business days, treats the referred client as the Referrer's client relationship (no poaching of the Referrer's other services, no marketing to the Referrer's client list), keeps the Referrer informed of the outcome of the introduction, and tells the Referrer before the client's first renewal so the Referrer can stay involved.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party keeps confidential what it learns about the other's clients and business. Alimi does not share the referred client's security findings with the Referrer unless the client asks it to in writing (a broker often will, for the insurance application; an attorney or CPA usually will not).

## 8. Term and termination

One year, renewing automatically, ended by either party with 30 days' written notice. Fees already earned remain payable after termination for referrals made before the notice. **[Attorney review]** Survival and payment on termination.

## 9. Taxes and records

Fees are paid to the Referrer's firm, which is responsible for its own taxes; Alimi issues the tax forms the law requires. Alimi keeps records of referrals and payments for seven years and shows the Referrer its own records on request.

## 10. Liability, governing law

Neither party is liable to the other for the referred client's decisions or for the outcome of Alimi's services. Massachusetts law; disputes between the principals first. **[Attorney review]**

## Schedules

- **A.** Referrer's fee option (section 4), bank or charity details.
- **B.** Client disclosure form: one paragraph the Referrer sends the client before the introduction, naming the fee or the discount.
- **C.** Alimi's current one-pager and price list.
