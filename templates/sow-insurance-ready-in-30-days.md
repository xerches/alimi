## 1. Objective

Within 30 days, take {{Client}}'s {{Tenant}} environment from its current state to one where every question on a standard cyber insurance application can be answered "yes" with evidence, and hand that evidence to the Client's broker.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** everything in the Workspace Security Scan SOW, plus: a prioritised fix plan; the configuration changes that resolve the findings (multi-factor authentication enforcement, administrator account clean-up, disabling legacy sign-in, blocking external forwarding, tightening sharing, enabling audit logging, email authentication records); one incident response tabletop exercise (90 minutes) with the owner and the people who would handle an incident; a rescan; and an evidence pack formatted for the broker.

**Out of scope:** endpoint protection deployment, backups, buying licences or tools, work on other tenants, policies beyond the incident response plan used in the tabletop, and negotiating with the insurer. Where a finding needs a licence upgrade or a tool the Client does not have, Alimi names it and the Client decides.

## 3. Deliverables

{{pricing.products.services.insurance_ready_30.includes}}

Plus: the fix plan with owners and dates; a change log of every setting changed, by whom and when; the tabletop summary with actions; the rescan report; and the evidence pack sent to the broker named by the Client.

## 4. Approach and timeline

| Week | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Scan, report, fix plan agreed with {{Client Contact}}. Changes that need staff communication (multi-factor rollout) are announced. |
| 2 and 3 | Changes made in order of severity. Each change is made by the Client's administrator or managed service provider on Alimi's instructions, with Alimi verifying the result, unless the Client grants Alimi temporary write access under a separate written authorization. **[Attorney review]** Authorization and responsibility wording for configuration changes made by Alimi. |
| 4 | Tabletop exercise, rescan, evidence pack delivered to the broker, access removed. |

## 5. Client responsibilities

An administrator (in-house or the managed service provider) available for about four hours a week during weeks 2 and 3; a decision-maker who can approve changes that affect staff within one business day; staff informed of the multi-factor rollout; the broker's name and email; screenshots for settings the scanner cannot read.

## 6. Access and data

Read-only access as in the Workspace Security Scan SOW. Write access, if the Client chooses to grant it, is limited to the settings in the fix plan, time-boxed to weeks 2 and 3, logged, and removed by the Client's administrator at the end of week 3.

## 7. Assumptions

One tenant, up to 200 accounts; the Client has or buys the licences the fixes require (for most small businesses, the business tier they already pay for); users cooperate with multi-factor enrolment; the incident response plan used in the tabletop is the Client's own or the template from Alimi's policy library.

Alimi does not control the insurer's decision. The service produces answers and evidence; coverage, exclusions and premium are the insurer's call.
