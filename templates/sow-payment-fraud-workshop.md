*Under the Master Services Agreement between {{Alimi Entity}} ("Alimi") and {{Client}} dated {{Effective Date}}. SOW number: ______. Prices are read from Alimi's price list at signing.*

## 1. Objective

Make it a habit at {{Client}} that no payment goes to a new or changed destination without a verified callback, two people and a 24-hour hold, so that a fake invoice, a fake executive or a fake wire instruction does not cost the company money.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** a half-day (three and a half hour) workshop for the finance team, or for a closing office its attorneys, paralegals and closers, up to twelve people, on site in the Boston area or remote; the Payment Verification Procedure tailored to the Client's payment systems, approval limits and roles; the callback scripts, poster and training materials from Alimi's Payment Fraud Protection Kit filled in for the Client; and one drill within twelve months of the workshop.

**Out of scope:** changes to banking products or accounting software, insurance claims, recovery of funds already lost, and legal advice on client funds or trust accounting.

## 3. Deliverables

{{pricing.products.services.fraud_workshop.includes}}

In detail: the free {{pricing.products.free.fraud_procedure.name}} with its callback scripts, poster and closing-day checklist; the tailored procedure as DOCX and PDF with the Client's threshold, roles and bank fraud line; the poster; the callback scripts; the 20-minute training recording or slides for staff who missed the session; a one-page summary of the drill with what was caught and what to improve.

## 4. Approach and timeline

| When | Step |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | 45-minute scoping call: payment flows, systems, approval limits, past near misses. Draft procedure sent for comment. |
| Week 2 or 3 | Workshop: the three rules, live callback practice using the scripts, red-flag exercise, agreement on the threshold and roles, sign-off of the procedure by the owner. |
| Within 2 weeks after | Final documents delivered; poster printed by the Client. |
| Within 12 months | Drill: a realistic change request agreed in advance with {{Client Contact}} and sent to the finance team; summary delivered within a week. Nobody is named. |

## 5. Client responsibilities

The owner or managing partner attends the first hour and signs the procedure; the finance team attends the whole workshop; the Client provides its approval limits, bank contact details and payment systems in advance; the Client decides on the threshold and tells staff that the owner will follow the boss rule.

## 6. Access and data

No system access is needed. Alimi receives the Client's payment process description and approval limits, treated as Confidential Information under the Master Services Agreement, and does not receive bank account numbers.

## 7. Assumptions

Up to twelve participants; one workshop; one drill; travel within the Boston area included, further travel at cost with prior agreement. A second session for a second team is a change order.
