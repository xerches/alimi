# Statement of Work: {{pricing.products.ai_policy_kit.implementation.name}}

*Under the Master Services Agreement between {{alimi Entity}} ("alimi") and {{Client}} dated {{Effective Date}}. SOW number: ______. Prices are read from alimi's price list at signing.*

## 1. Objective

Give {{Client}} a clear, adopted position on how staff may use AI tools, enforce the parts that can be enforced in {{Tenant}}, train staff in under an hour, and, where the Client needs it, show how far it is from ISO 42001 so that it can answer customers who ask.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** the AI Acceptable Use Policy and AI Governance Policy from alimi's library tailored to the Client; the one-page staff guide; an inventory of the AI tools actually in use, built from a read-only review of {{Tenant}} audit logs and sign-in records plus a short staff survey; a leadership session (90 minutes) to decide which tools are approved for which data and to adopt the policy; configuration of the access rules the decisions require in {{Tenant}} (blocking or allowing specific applications, consent settings, data loss prevention rules where the licence allows); a 45-minute staff training; and, if selected in section 8, an ISO 42001 gap check against the clauses and Annex A controls with a prioritised list.

**Out of scope:** building or evaluating AI systems, legal advice on AI regulation, contract negotiation with AI vendors, and tools outside {{Tenant}}'s visibility (personal devices and accounts are covered by policy, not by configuration).

## 3. Deliverables

{{pricing.products.ai_policy_kit.implementation.includes}}

Plus: the AI tool inventory with personal versus company accounts and the data each tool may receive; the approved-tool list; the training slides and recording; the ISO 42001 gap list if selected.

## 4. Approach and timeline

| When | Step |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Read-only review of audit logs and staff survey; inventory and draft policies sent. |
| Week 2 | Leadership session; decisions recorded; policies adopted and signed by the owner. |
| Week 3 | Access rules configured by the Client's administrator on alimi's instructions and verified; staff training delivered; guide published. |
| Week 4 (if selected) | ISO 42001 gap check delivered and walked through in 60 minutes. |

## 5. Client responsibilities

Owner and department heads attend the leadership session and make the approval decisions; an administrator creates the read-only access and makes the configuration changes; staff complete the five-minute survey and attend the training; the Client's attorney reviews the policy sections marked for legal review.

## 6. Access and data

Read-only access to {{Tenant}} audit logs and application records, created by the Client's administrator with alimi's published permissions and removed after week 1. alimi does not read the content of prompts, email or files; the inventory is built from which applications were used, by whom and when. Survey answers are kept confidential to alimi and the owner.

## 7. Assumptions

Up to 200 accounts, one tenant; the Client's licence tier supports the access rules chosen (where it does not, alimi names the gap and the policy covers it); one leadership session and one training session, recorded for absentees.
