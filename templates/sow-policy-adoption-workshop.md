# Statement of Work: {{pricing.products.services.policy_adoption.name}}

*Under the Master Services Agreement between {{alimi Entity}} ("alimi") and {{Client}} dated {{Effective Date}}. SOW number: ______. Prices are read from alimi's price list at signing.*

## 1. Objective

Leave {{Client}} with a set of security and company policies that fit how it actually works, are approved and signed by the owner, are published where staff can find them, and have a working record of who has read them, so that an insurer, a customer or an auditor can be shown the policies and the acknowledgements on request.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** the full alimi policy library (included in every tier of the {{pricing.products.platform.product_name}}, provided here as documents) filled with the Client's names and roles; a half-day workshop with the owner and the people who will own the policies to choose which policies apply, tailor the statements that do not fit, and decide the open points (thresholds, review cycles, who approves exceptions); a written list of the sections marked for HR or legal review and what each needs; final DOCX and PDF copies; the coverage matrix showing which SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, HIPAA and insurer requirements each policy addresses; and acknowledgement tracking set up in {{Tenant}} (a form or document library with a sign-off record) or in the alimi platform.

**Out of scope:** legal review of the marked sections (the Client's attorney or HR adviser does this; alimi lists what they need to look at), writing procedures below the policy level, and technical configuration except the acknowledgement tracking itself.

## 3. Deliverables

{{pricing.products.services.policy_adoption.includes}}

Plus the HR and legal review list, the coverage matrix, and a one-page roll-out plan: who publishes what, when staff are told, and when the first acknowledgement round closes.

## 4. Approach and timeline

| When | Step |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | 45-minute scoping call; alimi fills the library with the Client's roles and sends the drafts. |
| Week 2 | Half-day workshop; decisions recorded; tailored drafts revised within five business days. |
| Week 3 | Owner approves and signs; alimi sets up the acknowledgement tracking and shows {{Client Contact}} how to run a round and export the record. |
| Week 4 | Policies published; first acknowledgement round opened by the Client. |

## 5. Client responsibilities

Owner attends the first and last half hour of the workshop and signs the approved policies; policy owners (finance, HR, IT or the managed service provider) attend the parts that concern them; the Client's attorney or HR adviser reviews the marked sections before adoption; an administrator grants alimi the access needed to set up the acknowledgement tracking, or does it on alimi's instructions.

## 6. Access and data

alimi receives the Client's organisation chart, role names and existing policies, treated as Confidential Information. Setting up acknowledgement tracking may need a short period of limited administrative access to {{Tenant}}, created and removed by the Client's administrator and logged; alimi's preferred route is to instruct the administrator rather than hold the access.

## 7. Assumptions

Up to 23 policies; one workshop; one revision round; the Client's own legal and HR review happens within the timeline. Policies map to framework requirements by criterion ID; alimi does not provide the text of the standards, which are copyrighted, and the matrix is alimi's view, not an auditor's opinion.
