# Statement of Work: {{pricing.retainers.fractional_security_lead.name}}

*Under the Master Services Agreement between {{Alimi Entity}} ("Alimi") and {{Client}} dated {{Effective Date}}. SOW number: ______. Prices are read from Alimi's price list at signing.*

## 1. Objective

Give {{Client}} the security and compliance lead it cannot hire: a named person who owns the security program part time, keeps the controls working, answers the insurer and the customers, and makes the scoping and policy calls that tools leave to the owner.

## 2. Scope

**Included each month, within the hours below:**

- Monthly run of the workspace scan and review of the results with the owner; tracking of fixes.
- Ownership of the policy set: reviews on schedule, updates when the business changes, acknowledgement rounds.
- Vendor security reviews for new suppliers and the yearly review of critical ones.
- Answers to insurer applications and customer security questionnaires, with evidence.
- Access reviews with managers; joiner and leaver checks.
- Phishing simulation and the yearly training.
- First point of contact for suspected incidents during business hours (Eastern Time), running the response under the Client's incident response plan.
- A quarterly written report to the owner: what changed, open risks, what is next.

**Out of scope:** 24-hour on-call, hands-on IT administration beyond verification, software development, legal advice, audits that require independence from the person who runs the program, and travel beyond the Boston area. Incident response beyond the included hours is billed as extra hours.

## 3. Deliverables

The quarterly report; the monthly scan findings; maintained policies and the acknowledgement record; completed questionnaires and evidence packs; a risk register kept current; an annual plan agreed with the owner.

## 4. How it runs

A standing 30-minute call with the owner every two weeks; a shared tracker of actions; a direct channel (email and phone) for questions; response to a suspected incident within two business hours. Hours are logged and reported monthly. Hours not used in a month carry over for one month and then lapse; extra hours are agreed in advance at {{Fee}} per hour.

## 5. Client responsibilities

An owner or operations lead who meets Alimi every two weeks and makes decisions; an administrator or managed service provider who makes changes Alimi recommends; staff who complete training and acknowledgements; prompt reporting of incidents and business changes (new systems, new offices, new data types).

## 6. Access and data

Read-only access to {{Tenant}} for the monthly scan and reviews, created by the Client's administrator; access to the policy and tracker documents; no standing administrative access. Data handling follows section 5 of the Master Services Agreement. Alimi's access is reviewed with the Client every six months and removed at the end of the retainer.

## 7. Assumptions

Up to 200 staff and one primary tenant; the Client has, or buys under separate SOWs, the scan, policies and procedures the program runs on; Alimi acts as adviser and coordinator, and the Client remains responsible for its systems and decisions.
