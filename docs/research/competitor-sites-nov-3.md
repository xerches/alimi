# How Drata, Vanta and Alimi sell on their sites (Nov 3, 2026)

The three-way comparison the founder asked for after the two competitor site reviews: Drata (sixteen of the founder's captures of drata.com, review at https://claude.ai/artifact/MDShVzQWbzRQUz4aLccD7E) and Vanta (eleven captures plus twenty-nine pages read directly, review at https://claude.ai/artifact/Cmrg6sG4ZaodBhvGmMZmPQ). "Alimi today" is the site as it stands with PRs #18, #21, #24 and #28 applied; "Alimi proposed" is what the reviews suggest, each item numbered in the decision list below. Nothing in the proposed column is built until the founder says yes to its number. This file is the single source for the Compare page's "how each site sells" rows and for the prompts that build the options; re-read it before changing either. Companion to `vanta-drata-site-scan.md` (Nov 2) and `vanta-drata-complaints.md`.

Everything about Drata and Vanta is as their sites read on Nov 3, 2026. Drata's and Vanta's statistics are their own claims; Vanta's headline percentages come from an IDC white paper Vanta sponsored (January 2025).

## Decision list for the founder

Each line is a yes or no. The recommendation is the reviewer's; the rule conflicts are marked so nothing slips past the Nov 2 decisions (no free resources, no policy text in public, one number on the site, honest claims).

From the Drata review:

- **D1. Module pages** on a short version of the shared template, with screenshots from the demo (controls, a failing test with its fix, a policy with acknowledgements, the trust page, the export pack), one page per module under /platform/. Recommended: yes, after the demo deployment is fixed so the screenshots show the current build.
- **D2. "See the product" as the main header button**, Contact stays in the menu. Recommended: yes, once alimi-demo.netlify.app serves the current main; today it serves a Nov 1 build.
- **D3. An email-only field in the home hero.** Recommended: no; the waitlist form requires a name, and the contact form now requires company and size. A short path to the contact form instead.
- **D4. One line naming the person on each size band** (the owner who got the questionnaire; the ops lead with two customers asking; the company with a compliance person and a yearly audit; several entities and a board). Recommended: yes; copy only.
- **D5. A "built on" band** (Microsoft Graph, Google Admin SDK, Netlify) in place of customer logos until the pilots. Recommended: yes, in the future tense, without GitHub.
- **D6. A yearly "What insurers ask" report** from public cyber-insurance application forms. Recommended: decide the rule first. It is free content, which the Nov 2 decision ended, and the forms are the carriers' text, so it would map question topics in our own words, never the questions.
- **D7. Broker and CPA partner pages**, each with its own one-pager. Recommended: yes; the referral one-pager in /templates has to be split in two first.
- **D8. A Trust column in the footer and an Accessibility page.** Recommended: yes, after the Resources page's two accessibility findings are fixed; the page says what is tested, not a score.
- **D9. A monthly "See the product" session** with a registration form. Recommended: later; it is a free session under the founder's name, which waits for the employer outside-work check and the free-resources rule.
- **D10. An auditor-independence statement**, and the Diamond line reworded from "the audit run by Alimi" to "the audit managed by Alimi with an independent CPA firm" in config/pricing.yaml, the Solutions page and CLAUDE.md. Recommended: yes, with attorney review of the MSA wording.

From the Vanta review:

- **V1. An FAQ block on each mapped framework page**, five questions in an owner's words, including "what does the audit cost" answered with the tier price and "the CPA fee is separate". Recommended: yes; it is the page element Vanta uses best and the one place a price can sit beside a framework.
- **V2. The per-client arithmetic on For resellers** (a 25-user Silver client at the Partner discount, what it includes, against the per-client prices MSP tools publish). Recommended: yes; Vanta shows its service partners no discount at all.
- **V3. Three copy fixes with no decision needed:** "Microsoft 365 first" and the no-agent sentence on Integrations, and the training module named on Platform and Pricing. Recommended: yes, in the next copy PR.
- **V4. One guide per mapped framework** as a web page in an owner's words, no download. Recommended: decide with D6; the guides are free content by the Nov 2 rule.
- **D11. Volume bands above 100 users**, computed with the pricing rule at 100 and 250 users. Recommended: yes, before any comparison at 200 people is published; announce to the product thread before the calculator changes.
- **D12. Integrations page to fifteen tiles**, the six planned connectors marked as planned. Recommended: yes, with the product thread's agreement on the list.

Leave out, from both reviews: agents as the headline, the space metaphor, a sales chat box, twelve-card grids, investor and technology partner programs, a podcast, a community program, sponsored statistics, and any number on the site beyond the framework count.
