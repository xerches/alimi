# Verify before you pay

## Before money goes anywhere new, pick up the phone.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Call a number you already had.** | From the contract, the vendor record or the website you typed in. Never from the email, the invoice or the voicemail. |
| **2. Two people, every time.** | Any bank-detail change, and any payment over {{Threshold}}, needs a second person who has seen the callback log. |
| **3. Wait 24 hours.** | New or changed bank details are not paid for one business day. Pay a small amount first. |

## Stop and call if you see

- A change of bank details, however normal it sounds
- "Urgent", "confidential", "just this once"
- A new email domain, a reply-to that does not match, or "use my personal email"
- Gift cards, crypto, or a payment app
- The boss asking by message alone

## The boss rule

{{CEO}} will never ask by email, text or chat alone for a payment outside the procedure. If you get one, call them. Refusing is the right answer.

## If money went out

Call the bank fraud line **{{Bank Fraud Line}}** in the first hour. Then tell **{{Finance Lead}}** and **{{Security Lead}}**. Report at **ic3.gov** the same day.
